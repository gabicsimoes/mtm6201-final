Past events at Plant Palette

We are proud to offer catering and events services, creating special menus for intimate dinners, seasonal celebrations and corporate gatherings. Every moment is planned with care, using fresh ingredients and a welcoming service that aims to bring people closer. Here, every event tells a story, an experience made with intention.

In our restaurant, we serve our regular à la carte menu, crafted for dine-in meals. For events, we offer a separate catering menu, designed specifically for groups, celebrations and larger portions meant for sharing. Two menus, two experiences, choose the one that fits your moment.

Plant Palette was born from the desire to cook with purpose, respecting each ingredient and the power of fresh produce. Our story is shaped by small harvests, shared tables and the wish to rethink food beyond labels, creating an inclusive way of cooking that truly nourishes both body and relationships. We believe every dish carries a story, and our begins in the soil, moves through the kitchen and reaches you with care and intention.
